## Problem

What use-case or limitation is this feature addressing? Describe the situation
someone is in, not only the solution.

## Proposed Solution

Your recommended approach. If you have a specific design in mind, describe it;
otherwise a rough sketch is fine.

## Alternatives Considered

Other approaches you considered and why you rank them lower.

## Scope

- **Affected repositories:** which repos would change
- **Breaking change?** yes / no / unsure
- **Consensus, cryptography, or wire-protocol impact?** yes / no / unsure

## Additional Context

Links to related issues, prior art, external specifications, or design docs.
